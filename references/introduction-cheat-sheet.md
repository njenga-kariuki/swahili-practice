# Swahili Introduction — Quick Reference

## CORE (same every time)

Mimi ni Mkenya, lakini nilikulia Marekani.
Kiswahili changu ni kidogo sana — bado ninajifunza.
Nipe mwezi mmoja, nitakuwa tayari!

(I'm Kenyan, but I grew up in America.
My Swahili is very limited — I'm still learning.
Give me one month, I'll be ready!)


## PROFESSIONAL VERSION

Nafurahi kukutana nawe.
Mimi ni Mkenya, lakini nilikulia Marekani.
Kiswahili changu ni kidogo sana — bado ninajifunza.
Nimerudi Kenya kwa kazi.
Nipe mwezi mmoja, nitakuwa tayari!
Kwa sasa, tafadhali ongea nami kwa Kiingereza.
Ninajifunza kila siku.


## CASUAL VERSION

Mambo! (or: Sasa!)
Mimi ni Mkenya, lakini nilikulia Marekani.
Kiswahili changu ni kidogo sana — bado ninajifunza.
Nimerudi nyumbani kwa mara ya kwanza!
Nipe mwezi mmoja tu, nitakuwa tayari!
Kwa sasa, ongea Kiingereza nami!


## VOCAB CHEAT SHEET

nilikulia Marekani    = I grew up in America
Kiswahili changu      = my Swahili (Ki-Vi class → ch-)
nimerudi              = I've come back / returned
kwa mara ya kwanza    = for the first time
kwa kazi              = for work
kila siku             = every day
bado ninajifunza      = I'm still learning
tafadhali             = please (formal)
ongea nami            = speak with me
kwa Kiingereza        = in English


## SWAPPABLE PIECES

Instead of "kwa kazi":
  kwa familia           = for family
  kwa biashara          = for business

Instead of "mwezi mmoja":
  wiki mbili            = two weeks
  miezi miwili          = two months

Instead of "Mambo/Sasa":
  Habari yako?          = How are you? (neutral/safe)
